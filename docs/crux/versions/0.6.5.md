# Crux Harness 0.6.5

## Done

- **Study from your phone.** Leave the laptop on at home and study from your phone's browser at school, or anywhere with internet. In the account menu (or search for it), choose Study from your phone, turn it on, and scan the code with the phone's camera. The laptop does the work and the phone is a screen: lessons, practice, notes, Hub files, marking schemes and study rooms all work from the phone. Each phone pairs once; the list of paired phones is there, with Remove. Keys, models and settings can only be changed on the laptop. The first time, Crux downloads Cloudflare's tunnel helper (about 55 MB). The link changes each time it is turned on. An option keeps the laptop awake while it's on.
- **Rooms for a class.** The host sets today's work: a short line and, if they like, a Hub paper at a page, which everyone opens in their own viewer. At each break, everyone answers "How did that block go?" with Done, Partly or Stuck; the host sees the counts once 3 people have answered, never who said what. The same as Crux on the web.
- **Phone layout.** On a narrow window or a phone, the left bar becomes a bar along the bottom and panels open full screen.
