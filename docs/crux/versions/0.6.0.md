# Crux desktop 0.6.0

The first public release. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

## Done

**Look and account**
- The Crux mark (the Southern Cross) on the app icon and the rail.
- Discord sign-in before anything else, with a week of offline use after a sign-in.
- Plans named after the Southern Cross, the brightest star for the highest plan: Acrux (top), Mimosa, Gacrux, Imai (free trial). The account menu shows the constellation with your plan's star lit.
- First run after sign-in: course, year and semester, your units, and CAT dates. "I'm not an engineering student, skip this" skips it on this computer. The account menu reopens it (Set up my units).

**Study Hub files**
- Hub files on the rail: a unit's notes and past papers, downloaded once to `Documents\Crux\Hub\<unit>` and reused after that.
- "From the Hub" in the paperclip menu and beside "Add notes or past papers" when starting a session.
- The tutor finds and opens Hub files itself (`hub_files`, `hub_download`): "give me all the CATs for EMM 312", "go through the course outline for EMM 302". When a file isn't there, it says so, names the files it saw, and offers to derive an outline from the past papers, to use the notes, to ask the Hub admin, or to add your own copy.
- Share with your class: your own file, posted to the unit on the Study Hub Discord after you tick the consent box. A file whose name matches one the hub has is stopped first. Shows progress to Mimosa and the contributor medal (10 posted files a semester).
- Top contributors: their Discord pictures, a drawn medal on those at the goal.

**Notes and study guide**
- Notes beside a session: the Notes chip opens a column of your notes, kept in your Crux account (Crux web shows the same ones). Select text in the lesson to pin it.
- Study guide tab: Crux's guide for a topic, the pages to read and worked examples, each with "Open at page N" in a Crux reader window.

**Install and updates**
- An installer with a choice of folder, Start Menu and desktop shortcuts, and an uninstaller in Windows Apps and the Start Menu. Uninstalling asks before deleting sessions and settings; the default keeps them.
- "A new version of Crux is out": the app reads Crux web's `desktop/latest.json` on start and offers the download from this repo's releases. "Later" hides that version.
- Claude Code installed with npm is found (it showed as "Not found").

**Fixes found on the way**
- The installer installed nothing. Two causes: links in the server files that pointed at the build computer (now real copies; the build stops if a link is left), and a Low integrity label inherited from the build folder (the build now resets it).
